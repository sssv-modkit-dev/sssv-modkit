About & controls

Original demos are authored from primitives under CC0. They are not extracted or randomized game models. Unofficial fan project, not affiliated with the game’s rights holders.

Left-drag: orbit. Right-drag or Shift/Ctrl + left-drag: pan. Wheel or middle-drag: zoom. Alt + drag: move a sheet part. Click a part to select; Shift/Ctrl-click or Multi-select adds/removes parts. Click empty space to deselect. Touch: one finger orbits; tap selects; two fingers pan and pinch to zoom. Focus the viewport for Home: reset, 1/3/7: front/right/top, arrows: pan, +/−: zoom, WASD: travel, Space: up, Q/E: roll, Shift: faster.

Game mode: exploded sheets are browsing layouts, not assembled animals. Vertex colours approximate triangles without a colour word.
