Optional Python bridge

The bridge saves working files on your computer and uses the local Python tools to build a ROM. It is a developer tool and needs the project's Python tools, Pillow and NumPy. From your local editor checkout, run:

python tools/story_editor.py

Then open this editor on the local bridge. Check again works when this page is served by that bridge.

Tiles 0-19 are shared by every level in the zone. Tiles 20-31 belong to the level's sub-bank, which may also be shared. Dialogue and terrain edits are shared locally. Save & build ROM includes your dialogue edits and painted sheets from this browser session. With the bridge, it includes the selected sheet and saved dialogue edits.

Tap or drag on the enlarged tile to paint. Choose Pick colour and tap a pixel, or right-click, to pick a colour. Replace changes that colour throughout the tile. Navigate mode pans with one finger and zooms with two. The sheet and zoomed brush scroll within their frames.
