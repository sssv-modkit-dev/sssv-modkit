sssv-modkit

Tools for recolouring and reshaping the animals, editing levels, and playing your own copy of the game in a browser. Everything runs locally in your browser.

  • Model Editor demoTake bundled animals apart, recolour, bend and stretch them.
    No ROM needed.
  • Level Editor demoMove objects around a bundled level.
    No ROM needed.
  • PlayRun your own ROM, including patched builds.
    You provide the ROM; nothing from the game is included.
