sssv-modkit

A browser toolkit for changing Space Station Silicon Valley: edit animals, levels, ground textures and dialogue, then build and play your changes. Bring your own US 1.0 ROM; your files stay on your device and nothing is uploaded.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a ROM?

You can explore sample models and a sample level without one. Building changes needs your own ROM.

What is uploaded?

Nothing. Your ROM and edits are processed and remembered locally in this browser. Use Forget ROM to remove the saved session.

Which ROM works?

Space Station Silicon Valley, US 1.0. Choose an unzipped .z64, .v64 or .n64 file.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes, you can try Browser Play with touch controls. Emulation is experimental and speed depends on your phone and browser.

Where are the guides?

Start with Getting started, then follow Models, Textures & Tilesets, Levels, Dialogue and Play. Each guide has steps, a screenshot and limits.
