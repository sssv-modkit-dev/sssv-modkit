Frequently asked questions

Do I need a ROM?

Yes. You must provide your own ROM file for the game.
In the meantime, if you don't have one you can still continue the tour.

What is uploaded?

Nothing. Your ROM and edits are processed and remembered locally in this browser. Use Forget ROM to remove the saved session.

Which ROM works?

Space Station Silicon Valley, US 1.0. Choose an unzipped .z64, .v64 or .n64 file.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes, the tools and emulator include mobile play and touch controls.

Where are the guides?

Start with Getting started, then follow Models, Textures & Tilesets, Levels, Dialogue and Play. Each guide has steps, a screenshot and limits.
