Parts & display modes

Viewport & tools

Alt + drag a part to reposition it on the camera-facing plane. Choosing left/right-drag overrides that camera gesture on parts. Double-click to edit its mesh.

Viewing notes & controls

Triangles without their own colour word use mesh vertex colours as an approximation. Disable vertex colours to show them grey.

Exploded sheets are browsing layouts, not assembled animals.

Left-drag: orbit. Right-drag or Shift/Ctrl + left-drag: pan. Wheel or middle-drag: zoom. Alt + drag: move a sheet part. Touch: one finger orbits; two fingers pan and pinch to zoom. Focus the viewport for F: frame, Home: reset, 1/3/7: front/right/top, arrows: pan, +/−: zoom, WASD: travel, Space: up, Q/E: roll, Shift: faster.
