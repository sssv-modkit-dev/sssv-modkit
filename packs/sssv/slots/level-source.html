Open your US ROM or a compatible modded build to view and edit its level objects. The file is decoded in your browser; no game data is included with this page or uploaded.

Choose a stock or compatible modded ROM to begin.

Select objects, change type, position and rotation, duplicate props, undo/redo, and save level edits or a project with a Model Editor configuration. The Move tool (or Alt-drag) moves objects; Shift adjusts height; Ctrl snaps to 64 units. Hold the right mouse button for mouse-look and WASD camera movement.

Exported JSON is an edit description. Build & Play builds a ROM and IPS patch in your browser and opens it in Play; the local builder makes the same bytes. Terrain is read-only, animal shapes are approximate, and portal targets need a separate runtime hook.
