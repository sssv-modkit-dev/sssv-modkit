# Pre-alpha v0.7 · 100% byte-matched decomp edition

Congratulations to mkst and the contributors on the matching
[Space Station Silicon Valley US 1.0 decompilation](https://github.com/mkst/sssv/commit/3e4d861eb662f4adeba01e8cebb6d7f8c9df7b35).

- **Level Editor.** The viewport redraws when the view changes. WebGL 2 uses
  tiled texture arrays and reuses prop and animal meshes across instances.
  Moving objects updates the object buffers while the terrain mesh stays in place.
- **Model Editor.** Navigation, theme, and ROM controls share the header.
  The viewport has compact Display, Camera, and Export controls. The
  Workbench opens on the main part, puts Scale, Move, and Rotate in compact
  rows, and keeps Deform available under its own disclosure.
- **Phone layout.** Animals, Parts, and Edit open in tabs beneath the viewport.
  Tap Camera pad for the expanded touch controls.
- **Release banner.** The dancing bear returns in the v0.6 palette. Texture,
  wireframe, and part highlights spread across the model as it dances.
