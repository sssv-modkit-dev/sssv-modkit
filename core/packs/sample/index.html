SSSV Editor

A fan-made level and model editor for Space Station Silicon Valley. It runs entirely in your browser and ships with original sample content.

  • Model EditorTake sample models apart, recolour, bend and stretch them, then save your edits.
  • Level EditorMove and add objects in a sample scene, then save and reopen your edits.

Unofficial fan project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the rights holders. Contains no game data.
